# ABASONPLUS — EDITORIAL POLICY (Bangladesh, verified 16 October 2026)

## Mission
AbasonPlus answers: **What is happening? Why does it matter? How can I verify it? What could go wrong? What should I do next?**
Bangla-first, practical, source-linked and useful to Bangladeshi readers. Attractive hooks must never distort facts.

## Priority beats (rotate; never make all posts identical)
1. **Government projects & locality opportunity analysis (25%)**: sanctioned vs proposed vs under-construction vs completed projects; DMTCL routes, economic zones, ports, airports, roads and PPP investments; what is actually verified and the public-source update date. Geography-specific impacts and price claims need transaction records, zoning, plot survey and flood/access checks. NEVER state "guaranteed land value increase" or imply a government plan already built.
2. **Land Share / JV / developers (20%)**: landowner-developer agreements, registered ownership, power of attorney, allocation, build start/finish, delay, legal checks, utility service, transfer and dispute risks. Check the Real Estate Development and Management Act 2010 and regulations; state limits and seek qualified local legal counsel.
3. **Structural safety & earthquakes (20%)**: HBRI BNBC 2020, PWD seismic manuals, structural design, soil investigation, supervision, quality control, inspections; NO DIY structural recipes or certainty that any particular material alone is earthquake-proof.
4. **Materials & construction decisions (15%)**: cement, reinforced steel, sand, aggregate, concrete, bricks/blocks, water proofing, paint, plumbing, electricity; standards, genuine certificates, traceability, laboratory tests, life-cycle costs; no invented prices, strength ratings or universal brand rankings.
5. **Land paperwork, public e-services & scams (10%)**: khatian, dag, mouza, mutation, mortgage, boundaries, title search, registry and permits. Cite official Ministry of Land portals.
6. **Housing usefulness (10%)**: rent agreements, defects, stormwater, maintenance, ventilation, handover, home fire safety and affordable improvements.

## Mandatory format of each article
- **Hook** with a specific useful question or verified development.
- **What is confirmed**: project name, place, stage, date, authority and source.
- **What is not known**: proposal/target ≠ guaranteed outcome; projected jobs/value ≠ realized outcomes.
- **Actionable steps**: real documents to verify, site visits, local authority consultation, professional engineering/legal advice.
- **Opportunity AND downside**: acquisition/zoning, government land, resettlement, flood risk, contaminated/industrial land, transport access, cost, disputes and delay where relevant.
- **As-of date + linked sources** for project status, legislation, codes or numerical figures.
- **SEO**: clear Bengali title, honest hook, 2–4 meaningful headings, unique slug, focused tags, natural question and answer.
- Before approval, check published-slug duplicates, source age and claim strength.
- NEVER reuse fake/outdated sources, clickbait fear or guaranteed returns.
- Do not misrepresent historical estimates as present status.
- Each time-sensitive story needs a `verified_on` and `expires_on`; after expiration the GitHub email generator MUST skip the entry instead of publishing dated claims as fresh.
- ChatGPT Daily Research Task is enabled to create a new source-linked editorial JSON file on GitHub each day, but its first unattended scheduled execution still needs verification. The fallback queue is finite and is NOT live research. If the daily source, quality checks or upload fail, do not call recycled content new; notify via the independent Publication Audit. Confirm primary-source dates before publishing new government announcements.
